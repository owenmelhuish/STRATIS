How STRATIS enters an organization

It begins with proof, in their own numbers.

STRATIS never asks for a leap of faith. The entry point is a historical data proof: the lowest-commitment way for an organization to watch the model reason across their own data and surface what matters, before anything goes live and before a single integration is built.

How it works · click through the steps
01Step 01

They share a window of their own data

A subset of the data they already hold, across the channels, campaigns, and regions they choose, over a past period they pick. It can be a single export, handed over once. No live connections, no access to their systems, no disruption.

02Step 02

STRATIS joins it into one unified data layer

Every source is reconciled into a single reasoning surface, the same one the live platform runs on, in order for the model to see the whole period as one connected system rather than a stack of separate reports that were never read together.

03Step 03

The model reasons across the whole picture

Against the use case they nominate, STRATIS reasons across channels, campaigns, audiences, markets, and time at once, surfacing the cross-effects and patterns that only appear when everything is weighed together, not channel by channel.

04Step 04

It returns findings as evidence, not opinion

Every insight comes back with the evidence it rests on, the reasoning chain that produced it, a quantified confidence, and the projected impact of acting on it. Each finding is traceable to the exact data underneath it.

05Step 05

We walk it through together

The findings are presented live, against the use case they care about most, in a working session with our team. By the conclusion of the trial, the organization understands the value that the STRATIS insights bring to their business.
