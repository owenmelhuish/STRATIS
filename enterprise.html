01 · The problem

The signal that should drive the decision is scattered, siloed, and never joined.

At enterprise scale, the evidence behind any decision lives across platforms owned by different teams, measured against different KPIs, surfaced on different cycles, and never reasoned about together. The patterns that matter most are the ones that only appear when those sources meet. STRATIS joins them into one connected substrate and reasons across all of it continuously, so your largest commitments are made on the whole operation, and every recommendation is one you can defend to finance and to the board.

Why this is different

Not a dashboard. Not a model with a marketing prompt. A reasoning system.

Dashboards display data. Generic AI produces fluent text ungrounded in your context. STRATIS is the category built to produce specific, auditable, you-configured marketing decisions.

01

Reasoning, not reporting

Dashboards show data and produce no judgment. STRATIS produces judgments: every output carries a recommendation, the reasoning chain that produced it, the evidence it rests on, and a quantified level of confidence.

02

Trained on real operator decisions

The model was built on a proprietary corpus of real marketing decisions, the data behind them, the actions that followed, and the outcomes they produced. That corpus does not exist in public datasets and cannot be replicated by a general model, however large.

03

Configured to you, and compounds

A brand context layer captures your strategy, audiences, channels, competitive geometry, and constraints, and every recommendation is filtered through it. It lives only in your environment, never trains another brand's system, and gets sharper the longer you run it.

02 · Trust & governance

AI velocity, under the governance an enterprise actually requires.

Human in the loop

Every recommendation requires explicit human approval before execution, and every executed action is reversible within a defined window. The decision authority stays exactly where it is today.

Auditable by design

Every recommendation is preserved with its evidence, reasoning chain, and the operator action taken on it, in an append-only log. Any decision can be reconstructed against the moment it was made and defended to finance or the board.

Tenant isolation

Your operation runs in a logically isolated environment. Your signal never trains another brand's system and never leaves your tenant, enforced at the infrastructure layer.
