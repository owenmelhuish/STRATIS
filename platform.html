Why this is different

Not a model with a marketing prompt. An intelligence built for the work.

General AI can talk about marketing. STRATIS was built to reason through it — on a foundation no off-the-shelf tool can shortcut.

01

Trained on real decisions, not just content

STRATIS learned from the actual chain of operator decisions, the actions they produced, and the outcomes that followed — not the marketing content scattered across the open web. It reasons in the shape of a senior practitioner because that's what it was trained on.

02

Memory that's yours, and compounds

STRATIS accumulates an understanding of your brand that lives only in your environment and grows with every interaction. It never starts from scratch, and your signal never trains another brand's system.

03

A loop that sharpens with every decision

Every recommendation, action, and outcome feeds back in — so the system gets measurably sharper the more it runs. Its advantage widens over time rather than wearing off.
